# BR-07: Support a consistent wind-down routine

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Business requirement |
| Owner | Product (fictional: Priya N., Head of Product) |
| Status | Approved |
| Refined by | [PR-21](PR-21.md) |

## Need

People who keep a regular bedtime routine report feeling more rested. NightJar
should help users build a consistent wind-down routine around the time they
normally go to bed, without making any medical or treatment claims.

## Success measure

- 30% of active users with 7+ logged nights keep their bedtime within 30 minutes
  of their usual bedtime on 5 of 7 nights, after 4 weeks.

## Constraints

- Wellness only. All user-facing copy must follow the [claims register](../claims.md).
- Works for users who travel or live through daylight-saving changes.
