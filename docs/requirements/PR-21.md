## Requirement

NightJar computes each user's **sleep window** from their diary and sends a
wind-down reminder ahead of it. The reminder adapts as the diary changes.

1. The usual bedtime is the median *local* bedtime over the last 14 nights.
   Bedtimes on both sides of midnight are handled (23:30 and 00:30 are one hour apart).
2. With fewer than 7 nights there is no usual bedtime (cold start), and no reminder.
3. The reminder is sent a user-chosen lead time of 30, 45 or 60 minutes (default 45)
   before the usual bedtime, in the user's current local time.
4. Quiet hours / Do Not Disturb are respected by delivering silently.
5. Reminder copy comes from the claims register.

## Out of scope

Real push notifications. The demo shows the next reminder on the web page.
