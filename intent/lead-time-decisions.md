# Decisions: user-selectable wind-down lead time

The people below are fictional, like the rest of NightJar's company. Each answer
closes a question routed from the spec or the plan review.

| # | Question | Answer | Who decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Does the lead change how quiet hours apply? | Keep the current rule: quiet hours are judged at the time the nudge is delivered. A longer lead can make a nudge silent or not silent; it is never skipped (AC3). | Priya N., Head of Product |
| D2 | Should the nudge text mention the lead? | No. The three register templates stay as they are. No register change in this work. | Regulatory & Brand |
| D3 | Who approves changing PR-21 item 3 and S-114 AC1? | Priya N. approves: "user-chosen lead time of 30, 45 or 60 minutes; default 45". Add a new AC for the setting to S-114 rather than a new story. | Priya N., Head of Product |
| C3 | Changing the lead mid-evening can lose tonight's nudge. | Accept. Support has been told. | Priya N., Head of Product |
| C4 | On the spring-forward night, a 30/45/60 lead can put the nudge at or after a 02:30–04:00 bedtime. | Accept for now and pin the behaviour in a test. A clamp is a separate follow-up. | Priya N., Head of Product |
