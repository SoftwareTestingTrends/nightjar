# S-116: Weekly sleep summary

**As a** NightJar user,
**I want** a summary of my last seven nights,
**so I can** see how consistent my routine has been.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1**: The summary covers the seven nights before today: the "night of" dates from
  seven days ago up to and including last night, using the user's current time zone for
  "today". A night belongs to its "night of" date as shown in the diary (a 00:15 bedtime
  on Sunday counts as Saturday night).
- **AC2**: It shows how many nights were logged in that window, the average time asleep,
  and the usual bedtime for those nights.
- **AC3**: Average time asleep is the mean of the nights' durations, shown in hours and
  minutes rounded to the nearest 5 minutes, for example "7 h 25 min".
- **AC4**: The usual bedtime is the median local bedtime of the nights in the window,
  each night in its own time zone. Bedtimes on both sides of midnight are handled as they
  are for the nudge (23:30 and 00:30 are one hour apart).
- **AC5**: With fewer than 3 nights logged in the window, no figures are shown and the
  summary says "Log at least 3 nights this week to see your summary."
- **AC6**: All summary wording passes the claims check.
- **Non-functional**: Durations are real elapsed time, so a night that crosses a
  daylight-saving change is an hour shorter or longer on the clock. The summary sits on
  the home page, above the sleep diary.
