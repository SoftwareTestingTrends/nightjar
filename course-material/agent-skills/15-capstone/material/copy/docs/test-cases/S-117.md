# Test cases for S-117: Export the diary as CSV

Story: [S-117](../stories/S-117.md). Traces to BR-07.

## Test data

Conventions from the team's test-data reference.

- **Users:** Sam (America/Toronto, the default), Lee (Europe/London), Ana (Toronto, then London).
- **Default "now":** Thu 19 Nov 2026 10:00, America/Toronto. Cases that use another "now" say so.
- **Standard night:** bed 23:00, wake 06:30 local. **After-midnight night:** bed 00:25, wake 08:00, and its "night of" date is the day before.
- **Data sets:** D-empty (no nights); D-week (Sam, standard nights for 12 to 18 Nov 2026); D-dst (one Toronto and one London night across each 2026 clock change); D-notes (one night per note N1 to N8); D-1000 (1,000 standard nights for Sam, D-notes repeated).
- **Notes:** N1 `Slept well, woke once`; N2 `Felt "rested" today`; N3 `Late dinner` + line break + `then reading`; N4 `Très bien 😴`; N5 `  padded  ` (two spaces each side); N6 `=1+1`; N7 2,000 × `a`; N8 empty.
- **Clock changes 2026:** Toronto 8 Mar (EST −05:00 to EDT −04:00) and 1 Nov (EDT to EST). London 29 Mar (GMT to BST +01:00) and 25 Oct (BST to GMT).
